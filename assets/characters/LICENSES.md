Style reference: the images in `/workspace/forge/reference/` were supplied by Hussain. They were used **only as a visual reference** for palette and design (deep purple robe, gold trim, lilac sheer sleeves, tassels, hairpiece). No pixels or geometry from them are in the assets. The modest design changes (high cross-collar, no slit, natural proportions, slippers) are deliberate departures from the reference.

Tools (not shipped, no license obligations on the output): Blender 4.2.3 LTS (GPL), glTF-Transform CLI 4.x (MIT), three.js r160 (MIT, used for preview renders), Pillow/NumPy.
