The files in assets/characters/ are trimmed, recompressed copies of
those bases (clothes, hair cards, normal maps, and facial blend shapes
removed). Robes, hairstyles, and ornaments drawn on top are original
to this game. Idle, walk, and dance are original skeletal clips on the
VRoid humanoid bones.
